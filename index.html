Privacy & cookies

A simple website. A direct conversation.

This SLICK website has no enquiry forms, accounts, analytics, advertising trackers or embedded social feeds. Its own code does not set cookies or store information in your browser. Images and other site assets are served with the site.

The hosting platform may process technical information, such as IP addresses and request logs, to deliver and secure the website. A private preview may also require the hosting platform’s own sign-in and essential cookies. Those services have their own privacy terms.

WhatsApp and Google Maps are external links and only open when you choose them. When you message SLICK, SLICK receives your phone number, profile details made available by WhatsApp, and the information you send. Please share only what is needed for your enquiry or booking. WhatsApp processes information under its own privacy policy; Google’s privacy policy applies when you use Maps.

For questions about how SLICK handles booking messages, retention, or requests relating to your information, contact SLICK on 07391 659345 or at 126 Stapleton Rd, St Jude’s, Bristol BS5 0PS. You can also contact the Information Commissioner’s Office about data protection concerns.
